% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Introduction}

State the problem plainly. Why does it matter? What is the
reader going to get from this? End with a short outline
of what follows.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Problem Statement}

Be precise. Define terms. State what you are solving, and
explicitly what you are \emph{not} solving.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Background}

Cover the prior work and concepts the reader needs to follow
the rest of the paper. Keep it tight --- only what is necessary.
Cite with \autocite{} for footnote references, or
\textcite{} for in-text author mentions.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Approach}

Describe your method, model, or argument. Be specific enough
that someone else could reproduce it.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Analysis}

Work through the evidence, proof, or results. This is the
core of the paper.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Example}

A concrete, worked example. Code, trace, diagram, or case study.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Discussion}

What do the results mean? What are the limits? What did you
leave on the table?

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Conclusion}

One paragraph. What was the problem, what did you do, what
did you find. Future directions if relevant.
